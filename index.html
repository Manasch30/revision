Evidence-Backed Retrieval Principles

Active Retrieval · Spaced Practice · Adaptive Difficulty

This application is an adaptive spaced-retrieval planner inspired by forgetting-curve research.

Rather than forcing a rigid 8-stage Ebbinghaus timetable, intervals dynamically recalibrate based on your genuine retrieval effort:

Forget

Could not retrieve / major knowledge gaps.

next interval = max(60 min, previous × 0.25)
Hard

Retrieved with significant effort and struggle.

next interval = max(60 min, previous × 1.8)
Good

Retrieved reasonably well. (Top grade; no "Easy" button)

next interval = max(120 min, previous × 3.0)

"The core principle is active retrieval + appropriately spaced reviews + adapting to recall performance — not an arbitrary promise of photographic memory."
